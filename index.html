The name

What is the Wolverine stack?

Wolverine is a nickname. Sellers chose it because the comic-book character heals fast, which says more about how the product is sold than about what is in the vial. In practice it means two peptides, BPC-157 and TB-500, sold in one mixed vial or as two separate ones.

BPC-157

A short lab-made peptide. Most of what has been published comes from cell and animal studies, with very little in people.

TB-500

Usually described as a lab-made version of part of thymosin beta-4. The human research is just as thin.

Worth knowing before you buy. Neither peptide is an approved medicine. Both are banned in competitive sport under World Anti-Doping Agency rules. I am not making health claims on this page and I will not give dosing advice.
